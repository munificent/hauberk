Just a quick note to record a design decision. One question I've had is whether
priest/deity features should be a class or a separate mechanism.

In most games, priest-like rolls are classes you pick instead of some other
class. I believe ADOM and maybe some other roguelikes instead make it a separate
orthogonal choice: you pick a class and separately you can pick a deity to
worship.

I like the combinatorial options the latter opens up, but I think I prefer the
former. In the universe of the game, class is pretty much your profession, what
you spend almost all of your time getting better at. If you are so pious that
a deity is going to grant you supernatural powers, you aren't just going to
church on Christmas and Easter. You are certainly an active full-time member of
the clergy.

That implies that deity worship is a class.

I think that simplifies the game overall and makes it easier to design classes
and priest characters that hang together both mechanically and in terms of lore.
